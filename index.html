Who We Are

Ruaka Friends Church is a Christian community committed to worship, fellowship, discipleship and serving our community.

We welcome people of all ages and backgrounds to join us as we seek to know God, strengthen families and make a positive difference in our community.

Our Mission

To spread the Gospel, nurture spiritual growth, strengthen families and serve our community with love and integrity.

Our Vision

To be a vibrant and growing church that impacts lives, transforms communities and glorifies God.
